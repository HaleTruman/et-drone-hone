OpenCV camera projection

PnP evidence and final pose replay

Camera projection consumes GeometryFrameResult.camera_calibration, gate_model, pnp_relative_pose_estimates, and camera_pose_estimates directly. Cyan is the selected raw PnP candidate, optional amber is the secondary candidate, and magenta is the final regressed pose. Prior selected raw candidates are yellow and prior final poses are green; secondary candidates intentionally have no history layer.

World 3-D uses exact logged frame/cycle association and the production void_ned camera transform. The browser renders the resulting ui_projection LOCAL_NED values as north/up/east and does not recompute flight geometry. Gate orientation is provisional; missing or mismatched logged camera-mount evidence suppresses world gates while retaining the logged trajectory.
